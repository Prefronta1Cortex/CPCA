# Synthetic routes: results

10 independent draws per condition, 80 cells, 3000 events. Per planted route and draw, the best pattern is the one whose members' relative times correlate most with the route positions (|Spearman|). Reported: median |rho| over routes and draws; fraction of (route, draw) pairs above the 95th percentile of the cell-permutation null; median number of components kept (shuffle null; in brackets the Marchenko-Pastur count, which over-counts for uncentred arrows); median label-free rank of the route's pattern (1 = would be picked first without the answer key; patterns whose members span under 60 degrees rank last). Conditions L0-L6 take condition H apart one difficulty at a time.
