Generated by `scripts/summarize_hc5.py` from `results/hc5/` (`scripts/run_hc5.py`). Design and rationale: `docs/benchmark_hc5.md`.

Each session's wheel runs are split in half three times; each method is fit on one half and scored on the other, then the halves swap (6 fits per session and condition; the shuffled control uses the first split only, 2 fits). Score: signed Spearman ρ between the order of the best output's members and their held-out time fields; p against a null that takes the best of the method's outputs under each permutation. Cells: entries are the median ρ over fits, then the number of fits with p < 0.05.
