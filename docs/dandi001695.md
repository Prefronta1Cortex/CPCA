# Complex PCA on DANDI 001695 (mouse, Neuropixels 2.0, novel linear track)

Code: `cpca/dandi001695.py` (loader), `scripts/run_dandi001695.py`. Results: `results/dandi001695/`. Complex PCA only;
other methods only if complex PCA improves on hc-11.

## Data

- **Sessions:** 15 sessions with behaviour from 4 mice (González & Vöröslakos 2026). Each has rest/sleep before
  (PRE), 24–57 min on a novel linear track of about 1.1 m (MAZE), then rest/sleep after (POST).
- **Cells:** 44–173 CA1 pyramidal cells per session, plus 11–141 CA3 pyramidal cells.
- **Place cells:** 11–72 per running direction (hc-11's place-cell criterion), against a median of about 24 on
  hc-11.
- **Provided:** sleep states and ripple times.
- **Sleep is short:** NREM lasts 15–57 min per epoch, giving 444–2,523 population bursts, against about 3,400–4,000
  on hc-11.
- **Units:** one file stores position in metres, the others in centimetres; the unit is taken from the span.
- **Independence:** sessions of one mouse are not independent, so results are reported per session and per mouse.

## Design (written on November 4 before running)

**Cells.** CA1 pyramidal cells are primary, as on hc-11. CA1 and CA3 pyramidal cells together are secondary.

**Answer key.** Place-field centres from running on the track (`cpca.hc11.place_centres`), per running
direction. The track is treated as linear.

**Events, cut as on hc-11** (`cpca.events.population_bursts`, windows centred on the burst peak):
- **POST and PRE:** NREM bursts, the same number in both epochs (the larger epoch drawn at random down to the
  smaller one's count).
- **MAZE rest:** bursts during immobility on the track (head speed below 5 cm/s).
- **Secondary:** only the bursts that overlap a provided ripple, where there are at least 200.

**Complex PCA.** The centred fit, with the rerun's read-out.

**Scores.**
- **Whole-track order test:** `best_map_match`, best output, best-of-outputs null.
- **Fragment criterion:** `cpca.fragment`, a 40% stretch with at least 8 cells, 1000 permutations.
- **Timing control:** 100 refits on spike trains shuffled among each event's active cells. The timing p is the
  share of shuffled fits at least as high as the real one, computed for both statistics.

**Replay content of the events.** The paper's Bayesian analysis (`cpca.replay`, on the same bursts) gives the
share of clear replays (both shuffles), the median place cells active per event, and the mean sequence score.

**Primary outcome.** The number of sessions in which complex PCA passes the whole-track order test and its
timing control (both p < 0.05), in POST against PRE.

**Secondary outcomes:** the same for the fragment criterion, MAZE rest, ripple-gated events and CA1 + CA3.

**What counts as decent improvement over hc-11.** hc-11 POST passes order plus timing in 0 of 8 sessions (the
one order pass, Achilles 10/25, has a timing p of 0.11). Improvement here means POST passing in at least 3 of 15
sessions, more than PRE, and in more than one mouse. Three or more of 15 by chance at 5% has p = 0.036.

## Results (`results/dandi001695/summary.md`)

**Replay content.**
- **Place cells active per event:** a median of 12 in POST, PRE and MAZE rest (3–26 across sessions), against
  3–12 on hc-11.
- **Clear replays:** a median of 3.4% of events in POST, 3.3% in PRE and 7.7% in MAZE rest.
- **No difference in clear replays between POST and PRE.** This may be because the mice ran the track on several
  days (sessions on consecutive dates), so it was novel only in the first session.

**Primary outcome, CA1 pyramidal cells.**

| Epoch | Order + timing | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| POST | **3/15** | M01 03/18 (p 0.001, timing 0.010), M03 06/24 (0.036, 0.010), M05 07/29 (0.007, 0.030); three mice; binomial p = 0.036 |
| PRE | 0/15 | – |

This meets the pre-registered bar (at least 3 of 15, more than PRE, more than one mouse), at the smallest margin
that bar allows.

**Secondary outcomes**, which do not corroborate it:

| Measure | POST | PRE | MAZE rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fragment criterion + timing | 1/15 | 0/15 | 1/15 |
| Ripple-gated events, order + timing | 1/12 | 1/12 | – |
| Whole-track order alone | – | – | 8/15 |
| Order + timing | – | – | 0/15 |
| CA1 + CA3, order + timing | 1/15 | 1/15 | – |

On MAZE rest the whole-track order passes in 8/15 sessions without timing, so co-firing carries the map there, as
on hc-11. Adding CA3 cells (median 16 place cells active per event) does not help.

**Reading.** A small improvement over hc-11 (POST 3/15 against hc-11's 0/8, with PRE at 0/15), but fragile. It
rests on three sessions at the edge of significance, and the secondary analyses (fragment criterion, ripple-gated
events, CA1 + CA3) do not reproduce it. More cells per event (about twice hc-11's) did not by themselves move
complex PCA clearly above threshold. The decoder also finds only about 3% clear replays in sleep here, no more
after the track than before it, which keeps the replay share low.
